## (A) Lead/lag: do ETH, altcoins and SOL bottom before, with, or after Bitcoin, in USD and in BTC terms?

### Takeaway
There is no single "alts bottom after BTC" rule. In USD terms, ETH bottomed with BTC in 2018 and 2020 but five months *before* BTC in 2022. SOL bottomed 38 days *after* BTC in 2022 because of FTX, *after* the February 2026 capitulation low, and 25 days *before* BTC's quiet July 2026 low. Pattern that holds in the local data: when SOL/BTC is still falling hard into a BTC capitulation low, SOL makes a later, lower low. When SOL/BTC is already rising into BTC's final low (a quiet low), SOL has usually already bottomed. In BTC terms, alt/BTC ratios kept falling for months after BTC's USD low (ETH/BTC fell 37% in the nine months after December 2018). The Altcoin Season Index stays far below 75 in these phases.

### Inferences
- Rules worth coding and testing:
  1. **"SOL/BTC into the BTC low."** If SOL/BTC is down more than 15% over the 30 days before a candidate BTC low (2022-11: -48%; 2026-02: -17%), expect SOL to make a later and/or lower low. Wait for SOL's own low. If SOL/BTC is *rising* into the BTC low (2026-07: +13%), SOL has likely already bottomed and the BTC low is the buy point.
  2. **Capitulation vs quiet low.** On capitulation days SOL fell about 1x (2026-02-05: -15% vs BTC -14%) to 3x (2022-11-09 FTX day: -42% vs -14%) BTC's daily drop. At the quiet July 2026 low SOL was already rising. This supports the hypothesis that high-beta alts undercut in capitulation lows but lead in quiet final lows. There are only 3 SOL observations, so it is not statistically established.
  3. **Confirmation of a sustained advance.** After the 2022-12-29 low: SOL closed above its 50-day SMA on 2023-01-03, above its 200-day SMA on 2023-02-20, and held 20 straight days above the 200-day from 2023-04-11. SOL/BTC first closed above its 200-day on 2023-07-13 and held 20 days from 2023-09-29. That was about 9 months after the low and just before the big October 2023 breakout. After the 2026-06-06 low: SOL/BTC was above its 50-day by 2026-06-15 and above its 200-day by 2026-06-29. SOL held above its 200-day from 2026-08-19, which coincided with the late-August breakout. — LOCAL
- Alt/BTC ratios can make lows 6–9 months after BTC's USD low (ETH/BTC 2018→2019). A rule that waits for alt/BTC to confirm (for example SOL/BTC above its 200-day and the Altcoin Season Index above 50) gives up the first leg of USD gains but avoids buying alt/BTC downtrends.

- The Blockchain Center index history (value at each BTC low) was not found.

### Inferences
- In 2022–23, SOL's sustained advance did not start at its USD low. The first +170% squeeze (January 2023) was followed by a -45% retrace to a higher USD low in June 2023 and a *lower* SOL/BTC low. The durable trend started about 8.5 months after the USD low (September 2023 SOL/BTC low area). It was confirmed by deeply negative funding (-21% annualized), clearing of the FTX overhang (orderly court-supervised sales), and SOL/BTC holding above its 200-day (2023-09-29 onward, LOCAL).
- Candidate rule set: (i) buy-zone trigger when SOL funding is deeply negative (below -15% annualized) while price makes a higher low; (ii) trend confirmation when SOL/BTC closes above its 200-day for 20 days; (iii) treat the first 60–100% bounce off a capitulation low as a possible squeeze, not the trend.
