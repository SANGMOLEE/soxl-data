## Q1. Per cycle: how long was the base after the low, what date counts as the start of the sustained advance, and what triggered it?

### Takeaway
The "ignition" (the move that did not later fall back below the low) came 47 to 329 days after the cycle low. It was almost always a sharp one-day or one-week breakout through the top of the base, usually helped by a short squeeze and a liquidity or regulatory catalyst: 2015-10/11 (China capital-flight demand), 2019-04-02 (a $100M algorithmic buy), 2020-03/04 (Fed "QE infinity"), 2023-01 (CPI cooling plus a short squeeze), and 2023-10 (spot-ETF hopes plus a short squeeze). Two cycles (2015 and 2022-23) had a first "false" ignition that held for months before a second, decisive breakout. 2019's ignition worked for about 6 months and then fully failed in March 2020.

### Inferences
- A codable definition of "ignition" that matches the consensus dates: the first daily close above the 200-day SMA that is followed by +50% before -20%. By that test the ignitions were 2015-10-10, 2019-04-02, 2020-04-29, 2023-01-13 and 2023-10-16 (all LOCAL; see Q2).
- Basing length after the final low varied widely: about 4 months in 2012 and 2019, about 1.5 months in 2020 (a macro shock, not a classic base), about 9 months in 2015, and about 2 months to the first breakout but about 11 months to the decisive one in 2023. The deepest-drawdown cycles (2015, 2022-23) had the longest second-stage base.
- Triggers were idiosyncratic. The common thread is a forced-buying or flow shock (short squeeze, a large single buyer, policy liquidity, an ETF/DTCC headline) hitting a market where sellers were exhausted. The catalyst cannot be predicted in advance; the price behaviour (range breakout, 200DMA reclaim) can be measured.

### Gaps
- No primary-source dating of the "start" for 2011-12 was found; the 2012 dates are LOCAL only.
- No source confirmed the exact Fed QE announcement date (2020-03-23) in the snippets; the summary was truncated.
- The 2015 Bitstamp low was $152.4 on 2015-01-14 (LOCAL). The 2015-08-18/19 flash-crash low differs by exchange; not verified.

## Q2. Published frameworks and exact rules: dates in 2015 / 2019 / 2020 / 2023, and false starts

### Takeaway
Price-trend rules (200DMA reclaim, BMSB reclaim, golden cross, Mayer >1) all fired near the ignitions but also gave false starts: summer 2015, late 2019 and early 2020, the 2022-03 BMSB reclaim, and in 2026 the 2026-04 BMSB reclaim, the 2026-08-19 200DMA reclaim and the 2026-09-08 golden cross. Adding "200DMA is rising" cuts false signals but delays entry by about 2-6 weeks. On-chain rules (MVRV >1, realized-price and STH-cost-basis reclaim, Hash Ribbons, Puell) cluster in the same weeks as the price rules in 2019 and 2023. Hash Ribbons fired early and more than once.
