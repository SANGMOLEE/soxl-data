## 1. What are the indicators and thresholds? (catalogue, with 2026 readings)

### Takeaway
In 2026 the "soft" bottom signals have fired (200-week MA break, Mayer Multiple 0.61, record realized loss on 2026-02-05, LTH-SOPR below 1 for about half the year, supply in loss exceeding supply in profit in June, repeated hash-ribbon capitulations), but every "hard" cost-basis signal that marked the 2015/2018/2022 lows is still untriggered: price has not gone below realized price (~$53–54k), LTH cost basis (~$49k), CVDD (~$46k) or electrical cost (~$49k), MVRV has not gone below 1, NUPL has not gone negative, and Puell has not gone below 0.5 (2026 low 0.53). The most recent dated readings (late Oct–7 Nov 2026) show a market that had recovered to "Hope" (MVRV 1.45, NUPL 0.30 at ~$78k) before the drop to ~$66.5k.

### Inferences
- Realized price at the cycle high was about 43% of spot ($53.8k vs $126k; MVRV at top ~2.3), far higher relative to price than at earlier tops. A drop to realized price therefore needs only a ~57% drawdown from the high, versus the ~75–85% drawdowns that took earlier cycles below it. The 2026 drawdown so far is ~54% at the July low ($57.7k vs $126k), i.e. it stopped just short.
- Because roughly 8.8–10.8M BTC were underwater at $58–67k, the supply-in-loss signals fired while MVRV stayed above 1: the cost-basis distribution is top-heavy (ETF/treasury vintage near $75–110k) with a large, old, very low-cost base keeping the mean at ~$54k. Mean-based thresholds (MVRV, NUPL) are therefore harder to hit than count-based ones (percent supply in loss), and a detector should weight them differently this cycle.
- Strategy's cost (~$75.5k), ETF average cost (~$82k), TMM (~$76k) and JPMorgan production cost (~$77–85k) all cluster at $75–85k, explaining why that band acts as resistance and why being below it has not produced the classic "everyone underwater" MVRV signal.
- Practical rule for the detector: treat "MVRV < 1" as a sufficient but not necessary condition; add ETF-era proxies (TMM/AVIV < 1 for N months, STH-MVRV extremes, supply in loss > supply in profit, LTH realized-loss share > ~40%) which have already triggered in 2026.
