## 1. What happened on 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-14 (and the preceding two weeks)

### Takeaway
The mid-November drop is reported as a macro-led deleveraging: a hot October CPI and a Fed governor floating a 50 bp hike pushed December hike odds to roughly 85-90%, the dollar hit a 19-month high, Nvidia's CEO resigned on Nov 12, and reports of Middle East escalation hit risk assets, while spot ETFs had six straight outflow days. Bitcoin broke $70,000 on Nov 13 for the first time since mid-August, with 24-hour liquidations reported between $0.87 billion (BTC longs only) and $1.6 billion (all crypto), and touched its 200-week moving average near $67,800.

### Inferences
- The sequence is: Binance hack (Oct 29-30) and pre-election de-risking, a relief bounce to about $78,000 on Nov 4, then tech weakness (Nov 5-6), hot CPI and Waller (Nov 10-11), Huang's resignation (Nov 12) and the dollar/Middle East leg (Nov 13). The macro repricing, not a crypto-native failure, is the dominant explanation in the coverage found.
- The liquidation totals are not comparable to one another (4-hour vs 24-hour, BTC-only vs all crypto). A fair statement is "roughly $1.2-1.6 billion across crypto in 24 hours, about $0.7-0.9 billion in BTC."
- No forced-seller event (exchange insolvency, treasury-company liquidation) was reported for Nov 12-14. That matters for Galaxy's rule that the one failed 50-week reclaim in history was tied to bankruptcies.

- Senate control after the midterms is reported inconsistently (one source says a Democratic sweep, another lists the Senate as undecided); no mainstream election source was reached.
- Exact CPI release date (Nov 10 or 11) and the Nasdaq/S&P closes for Nov 12-13 were not confirmed.

## 2. Who says the July 2026 low is the bottom, and who expects a lower low

The "not yet" camp is led by Benjamin Cowen (December window, possibly January 2027) and Rekt Capital (a weekly close below $73,000 preceded a move to about $60,000 twice this year); almost no named source found has published a fresh call since the Nov 13 breakdown.
