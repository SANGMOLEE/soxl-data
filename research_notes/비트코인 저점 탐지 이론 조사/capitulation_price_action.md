## 1. Wyckoff method: SC / AR / ST / spring / SOS / LPS, objective criteria, and how analysts label the 2015, 2018-19, 2020, 2022 and 2026 Bitcoin bottoms

### Takeaway
Wyckoff's accumulation schematic is the closest established theory to our findings (1)-(2): a high-volume, wide-spread selling climax, an automatic rally, then a secondary test or spring that revisits or marginally undercuts the climax low on lower volume and narrower spread. However, Wyckoff labels on Bitcoin are discretionary, mostly retail/TradingView, frequently hindsight, and analysts disagree on which candle is the "SC" (for 2022 most say June 2022 with FTX as the spring; for 2026 they disagree on whether the spring has happened at all).

### Cited Findings
**Definitions and objective criteria (education sources, secondary)**
- Selling climax (SC): a high-volume, wide-spread flush in which professional buyers absorb panic selling; a close well off the low is typical. Automatic rally (AR): follows as selling exhausts and shorts cover; its high is usually taken as the top of the trading range. — [TMGM Academy](https://www.tmgm.com/en/academy/trading-academy/wyckoff-accumulation); [Wyckoff Schematics PDF hosted at ProRealCode](https://www.prorealcode.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/WyckoffSchematics-VisualTemplatesForMarketTimingDecisions.pdf)
- Secondary test (ST): revisits the SC area to test remaining supply; volume is usually lower than on the climax with reduced spread. A successful test holds around the climax low; sources differ on whether it must stop at/slightly above the SC low or may be near/briefly below it. A test that breaks decisively below the SC low implies new lows or a longer range. — [TMGM Academy](https://www.tmgm.com/en/academy/trading-academy/wyckoff-accumulation); [LuxAlgo – Climactic Action](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/climactic-action.md); [TradingView – Accumulation: Wyckoff Events](https://tradingview.com/chart/EURUSD/xBjKhniw-Accumulation-Wyckoff-Events)
- Spring: a Phase C probe below range support that recovers back into the range. One source says the spring undercuts on unremarkable volume and its test should be quieter still; another says the test should show markedly reduced volume and narrow spread. Confirmation = a quiet test that holds above the spring low, followed by a sign of strength (SOS) on expanding volume. — [LuxAlgo – Spring](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/spring.md); [NexusFi – Wyckoff schematics](https://nexusfi.com/a/strategies/wyckoff-accumulation-distribution-schematics)
- Caveats stated by the sources: a spring is evidence of thin supply, not a guarantee of markup; volume-dependent events (climax and its tests) carry less weight when volume data is weak. — [LuxAlgo – Wyckoff Accumulation Schematic](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/wyckoff-accumulation-schematic.md)

### Inferences
- Our finding (1) ("finale" 5-sigma candle) maps onto Wyckoff's SC, and finding (2) (later marginal undercut on normal volume, higher RSI) is, almost word for word, Wyckoff's ST/spring criterion (lower volume and narrower spread than the climax, quick recovery into the range). The literature therefore supports the two-stage structure but gives **no quantified hit rate**; our 8/10 vs 2/13 statistic is new evidence, not something already published.
- Labels are scale-dependent and inconsistent with our dates: for 2022 Wyckoff analysts called June-18-2022 the SC and the FTX low the spring, whereas our 5-sigma rule picks 2022-11-09 as the finale candle and 2022-11-21 as the quiet undercut. Both readings are compatible if FTX is treated as a "spring that is itself a second selling climax" (a possibility one source names explicitly). For 2018 analysts label "December 2018" as the SC rather than our 2018-11-19 candle. Implication: an objective sigma/volume rule is preferable to named Wyckoff events for back-testing.
- 2026 through a Wyckoff lens, combining the dated facts: SC = 5 Feb 2026 ($60,033 low, -6.05 sigma); AR = rebound into the $80Ks by early May; spring/ST-as-lower-low = 1 July ($57.7K, -4% undercut). No source found applies SOS/LPS labels to the current November 2026 pullback (see Gaps); structurally, a pullback that holds well above $57.7K would be an LPS/back-up candidate, and a close below $57.7K would invalidate the accumulation reading in the same way BeInCrypto used $15,476 in 2022.
- Codable Wyckoff-style rule set (thresholds to be fitted on our data): (a) SC = daily return <= -5 sigma(60d) with volume >= k x average; (b) AR high = highest close within N days after SC; (c) ST/spring = a later low within [-x%, +y%] of the SC low with daily volume, daily range and hourly peak volume all below the SC values and daily RSI above its SC value; (d) test of spring = pullback that holds above the spring low on still-lower volume; (e) SOS = close above the AR high on expanding volume; (f) invalidation = daily close below the spring low.

## 2. Classical capitulation research in equities and its technical-analysis toolkit (Desmond/Lowry 90% days, IBD, Zweig, VIX spike rules, retest on lower volume, Bulkowski double bottoms, DeMark, RSI divergence, weekly/monthly RSI, Bollinger squeeze/"shelf then breakdown", Hurst)

### Takeaway
The equity literature agrees with our core structure: panic selling alone is not a buy signal; bottoms need (i) a climax or cluster of climaxes and (ii) a demand-side confirmation (90% up day, VIX reversal, double-bottom breakout). For Bitcoin specifically, the best-documented price-only signals are weekly RSI below 30 (four episodes: Jan 2015, Dec 2018, Jun 2022, Feb 2026) and the low-volatility shelf that preceded the November 2018 breakdown; published win rates for RSI divergence and TD Sequential on Bitcoin are either absent or rest on tiny samples.

### Cited Findings
**Desmond / Lowry "Identifying Bear Market Bottoms and New Bull Markets" (2002 Charles H. Dow Award)**
- 90% Downside Day: downside volume >= 90% of (upside + downside volume) on the NYSE; Lowry also requires points lost to be about 90% of points gained plus lost. Treated as panic selling; a 90% Upside Day is panic buying. — [Scribd copy of the Dow Award paper](https://www.scribd.com/document/369653221/2002DowAward-pdf); [CFRA whitepaper page (2008 version)](https://www.cfraresearch.com/insights/identifying-bear-market-bottoms-and-new-bull-markets)
- A single isolated 90% Downside Day has no long-term trend implication and often occurs at the end of short-term corrections. Declines containing two or more 90% Downside Days often generate a series of additional ones, spread apart by as much as 30 trading days; longer-term investors "should not be in a hurry to buy back". — [Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/369653221/2002DowAward-pdf); [InternetNews summary](https://www.internetnews.com/it-management/technical-analysis-90-downside-day)
- The reversal signal is a 90% Upside Day (or, occasionally, two back-to-back 80% Upside Days) following the 90% downside cluster, usually within a few days; in December 1973 the upside day came 14 days after the last downside day and the source considered that too long. — [TheDowTheory.com special report](https://thedowtheory.com/portal/special-reports/capitulation-the-ultimate-bear-market-low-indicator/); [TradingView "90% Days" script implementing the paper](https://www.tradingview.com/script/bKz4L0Mw-90-Days/)
- Desmond's own caveat: several clusters followed by a 90% upside day did mark bear-market ends, but in other cases the rally was only an interruption of an ongoing bear; post-2008 markets produce more 90% days (uptick-rule repeal). — [Schaeffer's Research](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/analysis/2015/07/01/the-meaning-of-mondays-90-90-down-day); [iTulip forum summary](https://www.itulip.com/forums/forum/public-discussion-forums/news/127-90-down-days-on-nyse)

**VIX spike-and-reversal**
- McMillan's "spike peak" buy signal: VIX spikes and then closes well below its intraday high / reverses downward (example: intraday 25.84, close 22.55 in Dec 2022). Presented as theory with anecdotal support. — [Option Strategist](https://optionstrategist.com/node/265)
- Man Group, top 20 VIX spikes over 30 years: when the S&P 500 fell in the week after a spike, subsequent returns were positive 86% of the time after the second week and 100% after three months; when the market rose in the week after the spike, gains persisted into the second week less than half the time. — [Man Group, Views from the Floor, 14 Dec 2021](https://www.man.com/insights/views-from-the-floor-2021-dec-14)
- SentimenTrader: a VIX decline of 35% within six days occurred on only 25 of 9,700+ trading days (used as a peak-then-collapse trigger). — [SentimenTrader](https://sentimentrader.com/blog/a-plunge-in-the-vix-index-and-what-it-typically-means-for-stocks)
- Critique: on a risk-adjusted (Sharpe) basis, buy-the-VIX-spike rules mean taking a lot more risk (Elm Wealth test, discussed by Moontower). — [Moontower](https://moontowermeta.com/vix-and-buy-signals/)

- Adam & Adam: rank 26 of 39, failure 16%, average rise 39%, throwback 67%. — [ThePatternSite – Adam & Adam](https://thepatternsite.com/aadb.html)
- "Failure" means failing to rise at least 5% after the breakout; it climbs fast with the target: 23% of Eve & Eve double bottoms fail to rise 10% and 32% fail to reach 15%. — [ThePatternSite – Trading Quiz](https://www.thepatternsite.com/TradingQuiz.html)

### Inferences
- Desmond's framework is the equity analogue of our findings (3)-(4): panic selling (our 5-sigma candle) must be followed promptly by panic buying (our 9-39% one-hour bounce; in daily terms a strong up day within days). A codable crypto translation: after a <= -5 sigma day, require within 1-10 days a day with return >= +N sigma and taker-buy share of volume above a threshold; treat a gap longer than ~2 weeks as "too long", as in Desmond's 1973 example. Desmond's warning that multi-climax declines keep producing climaxes for up to 30 trading days is consistent with our 12-146 day lag to the absolute low.
- Bulkowski's numbers apply to confirmed double bottoms in stocks; they say that once the middle peak is exceeded, 12-16% fail to gain even 5%, i.e. confirmation still fails roughly one time in seven. Bollinger's July-2026 "confirm above ~$65K" is exactly this confirmation rule, and the subsequent "double top near $66K" warning shows the breakout was contested in real time.
- Weekly RSI < 30 has n = 4 and coincided with the bottom zone each time, but in 2022 it fired in June, about five months and roughly 12% in price before the final low, and in 2026 it fired in February, before the -4% July undercut. It is a "zone" signal, not a pinpointing tool; our 5-sigma candle plus quiet retest sequence is more specific.
- The 2018 shelf pattern is testable: daily Bollinger bandwidth (20,2) <= 0.05 while price sits on a multi-touch support in a confirmed downtrend preceded the finale leg by 2 days in 2018. Because direction is ambiguous in general, it should be coded as "volatility-compression then break of shelf low => finale candidate", not as a standalone signal.

## 3. Quantitative / academic work on Bitcoin crashes and bottoms (LPPL negative bubbles, liquidation cascades, mean reversion after extreme-sigma days, regime-switching/HMM, drawdown duration, exchange and quant-shop "what the bottom looked like" research)

### Takeaway
Academic tools exist for dating regime changes (LPPLS negative-bubble indicators, hidden-Markov regimes) but none has a demonstrated, out-of-sample record of pinpointing Bitcoin cycle lows; the only directly relevant numbers are descriptive: prior bear markets ran 364-407 days peak to trough with 77-85% drawdowns, and forward returns after the worst days are noisy at 1 week to 1 month but skew positive at 3-12 months. Industry research on February 2026 documents a statistically extreme capitulation (-6.05 sigma; record $3.2B realized loss) while being split on whether it was the cycle low.

### Cited Findings
**LPPL / LPPLS**
- "Real-time Prediction of Bitcoin Bubble Crashes" (arXiv 1905.09647) applies the LPPLS confidence indicator to both positive and negative bubbles in daily Bitcoin prices (2017-2019). Negative-bubble indicator clusters coincided with several price valleys, including July 2017 and June 2018, but the authors note the indicator did not give effective warnings in every case. — [arXiv 1905.09647](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.09647)
- Gerlach, Demos and Sornette, "Dissection of Bitcoin's multiscale bubble history from January 2012 to February 2018" (Royal Society Open Science, 2019): LPPLS plus k-means cataloguing of bubbles (peaks, not bottoms). — [arXiv 1804.06261](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.06261); [PMC version](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6689597/)
- Wheatley et al., "Are Bitcoin Bubbles Predictable? Combining a Generalized Metcalfe's Law and the LPPLS Model". — [arXiv 1803.05663](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.05663)
- "Negative bubbles and shocks in cryptocurrency markets", International Review of Financial Analysis 47 (2016), pp. 343-352 (only the bibliographic record was retrievable). — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finana/v47y2016icp343-352.html)
- Blog (not peer-reviewed): an LPPL fit to the 2022 Bitcoin "anti-bubble" reports R-squared of 0.96. — [Stephen Perrenod Substack](https://stephenperrenod.substack.com/p/bitcoin-bubbles-and-anti-bubbles)

**Liquidation cascades**
- Single-author arXiv preprint (Ramon Marc Garcia Seuma, submitted 29 July 2026, not peer reviewed): seven major Bitcoin liquidation cascades on one exchange; 39 variable combinations tested; a price-based "critical slowing down" warning signature appeared in five of seven cascades, not in the two sudden tariff-driven shocks, and inverted for the August 2024 cascade. Conclusion as reported: even the strongest recurring warning signs cannot warn of an individual crash. ## 4. Volume-based tools (OBV, CVD, spot vs perp CVD divergence, volume profile, TradingView "capitulation volume" scripts and their rules, anchored VWAP)

### Takeaway
Popular "capitulation" indicators all reduce to the same three ingredients, a volume spike versus a moving average, an abnormally large down bar (ATR multiple) and oversold momentum/distance below trend, and none publishes back-tested accuracy. CVD-based "absorption" (spot CVD rising or flat while price makes a lower low) is widely used as a bottom tell but the evidence is anecdotal and, in 2026, mixed.

### Cited Findings
- "Capitulation Candle for Bitcoin and Crypto V1.0 [ADRIDEM]" (open source): flags a bar when volume exceeds an SMA of volume by a set multiplier AND the high-low range exceeds an ATR multiple; marker size scales with volume relative to the average. — [TradingView script](https://kr.tradingview.com/script/8ORcHHqk)
- "Capitulation Volume Detector by RhinoTradez" (open source): requires high volume and a sharp price drop together; tunable volume multiplier, drop threshold and lookback; alert support. — [TradingView script](https://vn.tradingview.com/script/mF7M082X-Capitulation-Volume-Detector-by-RhinoTradez)
- "Capitulation Finder" by MChiestes (closed source): describes capitulation as extreme momentum + distance from trend + massive volume; bullish capitulation = RSI at/below oversold, price well below a moving average, high volume. — [TradingView script](https://in.tradingview.com/script/Q6OZBrqR)
- A "Smart Money Volume Matrix" script distinguishes high volume at lows (capitulation) from low volume at lows (exhaustion). — [TradingView scripts search](https://br.tradingview.com/scripts/search/accumulation/page-16)
- A TradingView implementation of Desmond's 90% up/down days exists. — [TradingView "90% Days"](https://www.tradingview.com/script/bKz4L0Mw-90-Days/)
- None of these script pages gives back-tested accuracy figures. — same sources
- CVD: rising CVD while price is flat or falling is described as a classic absorption signal; the perp-minus-spot taker-buy ratio shows when leverage rather than spot demand drives a move. — [BGeometrics orderflow chart notes](https://charts.bgeometrics.com/orderflow.html) (attribution uncertain)
- 2026 examples cut both ways: commentary that sharp CVD drawdowns during declines have aligned with local bottoms once selling eased; a February 2026 analysis where spot and futures CVD both fell "without the bullish divergence"; a report of BTC dropping to $69.9K as spot selling overwhelmed perp bids; a July 2026 piece arguing the tape (delta divergence on retests) had not yet confirmed a bottom. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/key-bitcoin-onchain-signal-may-mark-btc-s-next-demand-revival); [Glassnode Market Pulse Week 7, 2026](https://research.glassnode.com/btc-market-pulse-week-07-2026/); [LiveBitcoinNews](https://www.livebitcoinnews.com/bitcoin-drops-to-69-9k-as-spot-selling-overwhelms-perp-bids); [Buildix, July 2026](https://www.buildix.trade/blog/bitcoin-bear-market-bottom-calls-vs-tape-july-2026) (attribution of individual sentences uncertain)
- Glassnode-style reading: spot CVD rising while perp CVD falls sets up a potential short squeeze rather than distribution. — [Glassnode Market Pulse](https://research.glassnode.com/btc-market-pulse-week-25/) (attribution uncertain)

### Inferences
- Our finding (4) (hourly volume 20-60x the median, 9-39% bounce within the hour) is a far stricter and better-specified climax definition than the retail scripts (which typically use a 2-3x volume multiple and an ATR multiple). The scripts' structure nevertheless confirms which features practitioners consider essential, and our daily 5-sigma return filter is the statistical counterpart of their "range > k x ATR".
- The "Smart Money" distinction (high volume at lows = capitulation; low volume at lows = exhaustion) is the same two-stage structure as our findings (1)-(2) and Wyckoff's SC vs ST.
- Codable CVD test for the quiet undercut: at the candidate final low, require price low < SC low while (i) spot CVD over the undercut window is higher than at the SC, (ii) perp CVD is flat/negative and funding <= 0 (shorts pressing into absorption), (iii) daily volume below the SC volume. Published evidence for this is opinion-level only, so it must be validated on our own data.

## 6. Are Bitcoin cycle bottoms V-shaped or double/triple bottoms, and how deep is the retest relative to the climax low?

### Takeaway
Published commentary describes Bitcoin cycle lows as multi-month processes with a first capitulation followed by a later, lower or equal low, not clean V-bottoms; the one source that quantifies it says the final low undercut the first capitulation by 40% (2014-15), 46% (2018) and 12% (2022), and MarketVector puts the 2026 undercut (July vs February) at about 4%. These comparisons use the first major capitulation of the bear market, not our "finale" candle, so they measure a different thing from our 12-146-day marginal undercut.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Literature and our study agree that the modal Bitcoin cycle bottom is a two-stage (W / climax-plus-test) structure; pure V-bottoms (our 2015 and 2020 cases) are the minority. The 2015 "double bottom" description in the commentary (January and a later revisit) differs from our record that the absolute low was on the January 2015 candle; in Wyckoff terms that would be an ST that held above the SC low rather than a spring.
- Retest depth has shrunk each cycle on the "first capitulation vs final low" measure (40% -> 46% -> 12% -> about 4%), consistent with the "each cycle milder" thesis. A reasonable testable prior for future cycles is a final undercut of 0-15% below the first 5-sigma climax low; anything deeper would be out of line with the 2022 and 2026 pattern but in line with 2014/2018.
- Because the published undercut figures are measured from the first mid-bear capitulation (October 2014, June 2018, June 2022) while our finale candles are later (January 2015, November 2018, November 2022), the two approaches should be reconciled in the report: the first capitulation is usually NOT the finale; the finale candle is the one that is retested only marginally.

### Gaps
- No systematic, sample-based study (with definitions and n) of V vs double vs triple bottoms in Bitcoin was found; all statements above are analyst commentary on four or five cycles.
- No published measurement of the retest depth relative to the finale (terminal) capitulation candle was found; our own 12-146 day / marginal-undercut statistics appear to be the only such figures.
- Bulkowski-style statistics on double bottoms in crypto assets were not found.
