## 1. Derivatives signals (funding, open interest, liquidations, basis, options skew/IV, leverage)

### Takeaway
The February 2026 low was a textbook derivatives capitulation (annualized funding −15.46% on 2026-02-06, 25-delta risk reversal −19.34 and put IV 95% on 2026-02-05, 30-day IV near 90%), whereas the July 2026 low was not (funding positive while price fell, open interest subdued, BTC liquidations of only about $87M on the break of $64K). In November 2026 the picture is partial: funding flipped negative on 2026-11-13 and one-week put skew reached about 14%, but DVOL sat near 40 and "barely reacted", i.e. there is no volatility panic yet.

### Inferences
- Two different "negative funding" signals are in circulation and should not be conflated: the single-day aggregate print (our own measurement: negative only on capitulation days such as 2026-02-06) and K33's 30-day-average regime (negative 46 days at the 2022 bottom, 63 days in 2020, 67–81 days in March–May 2026). The first is a day-timing signal; the second is a zone signal.
- By the February 2026 template (funding −15% annualized, risk reversal near −19, 30-day IV near 90, liquidations above $1B BTC-only), November 13 qualifies only on funding sign and liquidation size. The absence of an IV spike (DVOL about 40 versus about 90 in February) and of an OI flush (OI flat in BTC terms) argues that a full derivatives capitulation has not yet occurred.
- The July 2026 low shows that a durable low can form with no derivatives capitulation at all, so derivatives signals have a false-negative problem as well as false positives.
- CME backwardation fired at the November 2025 local low near $80,000, which was followed by a further fall to $60,000 in February 2026 — a documented false "bottom" signal for the basis indicator.

### Inferences
- Ranking by evidence retrieved: (1) zone signals with quantified records — K33's 30-day negative-funding regime; (2) day-level signals with anecdotal support only — single-day funding extremes, risk-reversal and IV spikes, $1B+ liquidation days; (3) lagging confirmations — Coinbase/kimchi premium streaks, Fear & Greed streak length, stablecoin contraction; (4) signals with a documented 2025–26 false positive — CME backwardation (November 2025), K33's February "maximum drawdown" call, ETF-holder-underwater (true since February, price went lower).
- 2026 as an out-of-sample test: the composite derivatives capitulation of 2026-02-05/06 identified a low that held for about five months but was undercut by about 4% on 2026-07-01; the July low was identified by none of the derivatives signals and by all of the flow signals (ETF record outflow week, Strategy sale filing, mNAV below 1, miner record sales, record reverse kimchi premium). A robust detector therefore needs both families.
- For 2026-11-14 the composite reads "early": one day of negative funding, skew at 14%, Fear & Greed 13, seven ETF outflow days — but no IV spike, no OI flush, no new Strategy/mNAV stress, and a macro backdrop still tightening.

- No quantified backtest was retrieved from Coinbase Institutional, Galaxy, Amberdata, Velo, The Block Research or CryptoQuant on bottom-timing accuracy of these signals; K33's drawdown statistics and methodology (sample windows, overlap) were not available in the summaries.
